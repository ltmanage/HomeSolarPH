---
title: Red flags
order: 2
summary: Warning signs that a quote or installer needs a closer look.
---

None of these automatically means a bad installer, but each one deserves a clear answer before you sign.

- **No itemized quote.** A single lump-sum price makes it impossible to compare or check what is included.
- **No site visit** before a final price, especially if your roof has shading or is unusual.
- **Very large upfront payment.** More than half the price before installation leaves you exposed if work stops.
- **Payback promised as a single, very short number** without showing the assumptions.
- **A system much larger than your usage**, sold on "future needs" without explaining the cost difference.
- **A battery pushed without a clear reason** when you use most power in the daytime and plan net metering.
- **Net metering "not our job".** Ask who will handle it and how long it takes; without it, exported power earns nothing.
- **Unclear warranties**, or warranties that depend on an overseas manufacturer with no local partner.
- **No workmanship warranty**, or one shorter than 2 years.
- **Pressure to sign today** with a "limited-time" discount.
- **Brands or models not stated**, or "equivalent brand" substitutions allowed without your approval.
- **No written contract** setting out scope, timeline, payment milestones and warranties.
