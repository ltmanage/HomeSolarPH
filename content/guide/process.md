---
title: Steps from quote to switch-on
order: 3
summary: What happens at each stage, and what you need to do.
---

The steps below are typical for a grid-tie home system with net metering. Details differ by utility and city, so confirm them with your installer and utility.

1. **Check your numbers.** Use this app's solar check with your recent bills. Note your monthly kWh and when you use most power.
2. **Ask for quotes.** Ask at least three installers. Give each the same bills and roof photos, and ask the same questions (see *Questions to ask an installer*).
3. **Site survey.** The installer inspects your roof, shading, panel board and wiring. Expect the quote to be adjusted after the survey.
4. **Compare quotes.** Use this app's quote comparison. Check size, price per watt, brands, inclusions, warranties and payment terms.
5. **Contract.** Sign a written contract with the scope, brands and models, timeline, payment milestones and warranties.
6. **Permits.** The installer usually secures the city or municipal permits (for example an electrical permit). Ask for copies.
7. **Installation.** Usually one to a few days for a home system. Keep the payment schedule tied to progress.
8. **Inspection and testing.** The installer tests the system. The city or utility may inspect it.
9. **Net-metering application.** Filed with your utility with the required documents. The utility evaluates the application and signs a net-metering agreement with you.
10. **Meter change.** The utility installs a meter that records power both used and exported. Only then do exports earn bill credits.
11. **Monitor your output.** Compare actual production with the estimate in the first few months, and keep your documents for warranty claims.

**Tip:** ask installers how long net metering is currently taking with your utility, and whether the system will be set to avoid exporting until the meter is changed.
