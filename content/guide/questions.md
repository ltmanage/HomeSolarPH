---
title: Questions to ask an installer
order: 1
summary: Ask every installer the same questions so you can compare answers fairly.
---

Ask every installer the same questions, and ask for answers **in writing** or in the quote itself.

## System design
- How did you choose the system size for my home? Which figures from my bill did you use?
- How much electricity do you expect the system to produce per year? How did you calculate it?
- How will you check shading on my roof (trees, buildings, water tanks)?
- Which way will the panels face, and at what angle?
- Will you do a site visit before the final quote?

## Equipment
- Which panel brand, model and wattage, and how many panels?
- Which inverter brand, model and size? Is it grid-tie or hybrid?
- If a battery is included: what brand, how many kWh usable, and what will it actually do for me?
- What mounting system will you use for my roof type, and how will you prevent leaks?

## Price and what is included
- Is the price **itemized** (panels, inverter, mounting, wiring, labour, permits)?
- Is the price inclusive of 12% VAT?
- Are permits, the net-metering application, and the meter change included?
- Do I need electrical upgrades (panel board, breakers, wiring)? Are they included?
- Is a monitoring app or device included?
- How long is the quote valid?

## Warranty
- How many years for the panels (product and performance), inverter, battery and **workmanship**?
- Who handles warranty claims: you, a local distributor, or the overseas manufacturer?
- What happens to the warranty if your company closes?

## Net metering and permits
- Will you file the net-metering application with my utility? How long does it usually take?
- Which permits will you get from the city or municipality?
- Can the system run before net metering is approved? Will it export power before the meter is changed?

## Payment and timeline
- What is the payment schedule? How much is due before installation?
- How many weeks from signing to installation, and to net-metering approval?
- Will there be a written contract with the scope, timeline and warranties?

## Experience and after-sales
- How many home systems like mine have you installed? Can I speak with past customers nearby?
- Are your installers trained or licensed? Who signs off the electrical work?
- What after-sales support do you give (cleaning, check-ups, response time for faults)?
